Terms You'll See in This Portal

These words come up throughout the case. Here's what they mean in plain language:

Express Trust
A trust created on purpose, with clear intent, a named trustee, and identified property. Ours holds equitable title to 120 77th Street, North Bergen, NJ — Block 301, Lot 17.
Spendthrift Clause
A provision in the trust that prevents outside creditors from seizing trust assets. Think of it as a legal force field.
Chain of Title
The documented history of who owns a property or loan. If a link is missing or forged, the chain is "broken" and claims based on it are weakened.
Securitization
When a mortgage is bundled with other loans and sold as an investment product. Our loan was securitized — and the rules for doing so were not followed.
JURIS
Our private intelligence library. It contains 781+ legal documents including the complete Restatement (Third) of Restitution, Cha0s equity doctrine, case law, governance docs, and evidence — all searchable by AI in seconds.
The Fortress
The five-layer legal structure that protects the family: PMA (constitutional) → RBT (fiduciary) → Private Bank & Estate (operational) → Express Trust (the property) → DeLorean (temporal anchor).
RAZOR
Your father's operational callsign. You'll see it on documents and in the intelligence files. It means the same as "Dad" or "Ramon."
Constructive Trust
A legal weapon. When someone holds property they shouldn't have, equity can impose a "constructive trust" forcing them to give it back. This is one of our primary tools.
