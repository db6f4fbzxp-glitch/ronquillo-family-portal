[ THE SCRIPT // WHAT RAZOR SAYS ]
I
OPENING — STATE WHAT YOU ARE

Your Honor — I am Ramon Ronquillo. I am the man who lives at Block 301 Lot 17. I am a father. I am standing here today because I have one question that no one has been able to answer for me.

Who do I pay?

That is my question. That is why I am here. I want to pay. I want to resolve this. I want my family to be safe. But I cannot pay if no one can tell me who has the authority to accept my payment and release my home.

STOP. LET IT SIT. LET COSTELLO FEEL IT.

II
FOURTEEN DEMANDS — PLAY DUMB

Your Honor — I have asked this question fourteen times. In writing. Formally. I have the letters. I have the dates. I have the certified mail receipts.

Fourteen times I asked — who owns this loan? Who can verify the payoff? Who can accept full payment? Who can release the lien?

Fourteen times — silence.

I am not a lawyer Your Honor. I do not understand all of these structures. But I know this — if someone is trying to take my home — they should be able to tell me who I owe. They have not done that. I am asking this Chancellor to make them do that before anything else happens.

STOP.

III
THE TENDER PARADOX — PLAY DUMB

Your Honor — I went to the post office once to ask about sending a money order. The man behind the counter said — who do you make it out to?

I did not know. I still do not know.

Lakeview says they are the servicer. But when I asked them if they could accept full payoff and release my home — silence. I looked up the trust — Ginnie Mae Trust 2021-116. I do not understand all of what that means. But I understand this — someone put my loan into a trust. And now no one can tell me who speaks for that trust. Who can accept my payment. Who can sign the release.

Your Honor — I am not trying to get out of anything. I am trying to find the door. And no one will show me where it is.

STOP. LET COSTELLO LOOK AT LAKEVIEW. LET SELMANOVIC SQUIRM.

IV
THE SUBPOENAS — PLAY DUMB

Your Honor — I did not know what else to do. So I asked the federal court to help me get answers. I served five subpoenas. I asked for forty-one categories of documents.

Not because I am trying to cause trouble. Because I am trying to find out who I owe.

Those responses were due May 6th Your Honor. I have them here. Or I have the silence. Either way — I would like to show this Court what I received. Because whatever I received — or did not receive — tells this Court everything about whether anyone can actually answer my question.

STOP. HOLD UP THE DOCUMENTS. OR HOLD UP THE SILENCE. EITHER ONE WINS.

V
MONICA CAPPS — THE KILL SHOT

Your Honor — I read the papers that Lakeview filed. There is a certification in there. It says someone named Monica Capps certified that Lakeview complied with the court rules.

I looked up Monica Capps. She works for LoanCare LLC. Not Lakeview.

Your Honor — I am confused. If Lakeview is the party asking to take my home — why is someone from a different company certifying their compliance? I am not saying anything improper happened. I am just saying — I do not understand it. And I think this Chancellor should understand it before granting any relief.

STOP. PLAY DUMB. LET COSTELLO ASK SELMANOVIC: "WHO IS MONICA CAPPS?"

VI
THE SUBSTANCE — NOT THE FORM

Your Honor — I also want to say one more thing I do not understand.

The company that collected my payments every month was LoanCare. Their letter says — "We acquired the sub-servicing of the account on August 1 2021." The website they sent me to was lakeviewloanservicing.myloancare.com

LoanCare's employee certified Lakeview's compliance. LoanCare's systems handled my account. LoanCare's people interacted with me.

But Lakeview is the one in court. Asking to take my home.

Your Honor — I am not a lawyer. But I know that in New Jersey — courts look at substance — not just form.

The substance here is — I do not know which company actually holds my loan. I do not know which company can accept my payment. I do not know which company can release my home.

The form says Lakeview. The substance says — I still do not know who to pay.

STOP. LET COSTELLO APPLY THE MAXIM HERSELF. EQUITY REGARDS SUBSTANCE RATHER THAN FORM. APPLESTEIN. FORTUGNO. SHE KNOWS THEM. LET HER REACH FOR THEM.

VII
CLOSING — BACK TO THE BEGINNING

Your Honor — I came here today with one question. Who do I pay? I still do not have the answer.

New Jersey courts have said — "Equity will not suffer a wrong without a remedy." Crane v. Bielski. 1954.

The wrong here is simple. Someone is trying to take my family's home. And no one can tell me who I owe. No one can tell me who can accept my payment. No one can tell me who can release my home.

That is the wrong. This Court of Conscience is the remedy.

I am asking this Chancellor — to require Lakeview to answer that question before anything else happens. Before any default stands. Before any foreclosure proceeds. Before my family loses their home.

Just answer the question.

Who do I pay? Who can verify the amount? Who can accept the payment? Who can release my home?

If they can answer that — we can resolve this. If they cannot answer that — then this Chancellor should know why.

STOP. SIT DOWN. DO NOT SAY ANOTHER WORD. LET THE SILENCE WORK.

5
THE FIVE ANSWERS — PREPARED RESPONSES

Q1: "THE DEED IS NOT IN THE TRUST'S NAME"

"Your Honor — equitable interest does not require a recorded deed. The Express Trust's equitable interest was established upon execution of the trust instrument on October 19, 2025. The clouded title is the precise defect preventing completion of the deed sequence. This Court has jurisdiction to clear that cloud under N.J.S.A. 3B:2-2."

Q2: "THE FEDERAL COURT DENIED YOUR INJUNCTION"

"Your Honor — Judge Farbiarz denied on procedural grounds in the context of a bankruptcy appeal. He did not rule on the merits. What he DID do — in ECF 13 and ECF 21 — is characterize this property as trust property. That is persuasive authority. This Court's jurisdiction under 3B:2-2 is independent of the federal proceedings."

Q3: "WHY DIDN'T YOU RAISE THIS EARLIER?"

"Your Honor — the Trustee provided actual notice to Lakeview on October 21, 2025 — seven months ago. LEFT WITH INDIVIDUAL. 1:28 PM. Lakeview's counsel confirmed actual notice in open court before Judge Sherwood on March 24, 2026. The trust interest has been on the record in three courts. This is not a late claim."

Q4: "WHAT EXACTLY ARE YOU ASKING ME TO DO?"

"Your Honor — the Trustee seeks seven orders. First — recognition of the Express Trust's equitable interest. Second — recognition of jurisdiction under 3B:2-2. Third — a stay pending federal proceedings and a full accounting. Fourteen demands. Fourteen silences. That is the accounting this Court should order."

Q5: "ARE YOU AN ATTORNEY?"

"Your Honor — the Trustee is appearing in trust capacity pursuant to N.J.S.A. 2A:50-13 — which expressly provides that it shall not be necessary to make the beneficiary a party to any action brought by a trustee. The Trustee is fulfilling a fiduciary duty to defend the trust res."
