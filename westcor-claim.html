⚠️ MERS SECTION 9 — “NO ADVERSE INTEREST” WARRANTY BREACH
MERS Membership Agreement Section 9 requires every member to warrant that no adverse interest exists at the time of registration. When the MERS assignment was executed, UBS AG held an active UCC-1 filing (Delaware No. 2019-6819697) creating a perfected security interest in the collateral — including this loan.
The Section 9 warranty was false when signed. Under UCC Article 9, UBS AG’s perfected security interest was senior to any subsequent claim. The MERS assignment was void ab initio — you cannot assign what you do not own free and clear. Nemo dat quod non habet.

👻 SIX GHOST POOLS — SAME LOAN, SIX SECURITIES CLAIMS
The same mortgage loan appears in six separate securitization pools. Each pool claimed ownership of this loan as collateral backing mortgage-backed securities. Under PSA (Pooling and Servicing Agreement) rules and the Trust Indenture Act, a single loan cannot secure multiple trusts simultaneously. If the loan was validly transferred to Pool 1, it cannot also be in Pools 2–6. At least five of the six claims are necessarily fraudulent.
