STEP 2 — The FRBNY Finding

FRBNY BIC INELIGIBILITY
Federal Reserve Bank of New York explicitly states: loans documented as repurchase agreements are INELIGIBLE for BIC pledging.

CNB could not pledge this note to the Federal Reserve under BIC.

The deeper question:
Did CrossCountry ever actually repurchase the note?

STEP 3 — The MERS Assignment Problem

VOID — NOT VOIDABLE
November 1, 2024 — MERS assigns the mortgage to Lakeview.

Under Restatement Third, Property (Mortgages) § 5.4(c) — you can only enforce the mortgage if you are entitled to enforce the obligation (the note).

MERS cannot transfer what MERS never had. The MIN was inactive. No nominee authority.

VOID. NOT VOIDABLE. VOID.
