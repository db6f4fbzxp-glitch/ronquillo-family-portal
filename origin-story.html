Part 1
What a Perfect Mortgage Looks Like
The clean chain — as it should have been. Six steps. Every link documented. Every instrument traveling together.
STEP 1 — ORIGINATION ›

Borrower signs TWO instruments:

INSTRUMENT 1 — THE NOTE: “I promise to pay X dollars at Y interest over Z years.” This is the DEBT — the personal obligation — the negotiable instrument under UCC Article 3.

INSTRUMENT 2 — THE MORTGAGE: “If I fail to pay — this property secures the note.” This is the SECURITY INTEREST — attached to the land — follows the note ALWAYS.

CRITICAL RULE — DAY ONE: Note + Mortgage must travel together. ALWAYS. FOREVER. They are one instrument in two parts. Separate them = destroy the mortgage. AmJur §909 — Assignment of mortgage apart from debt = NULLITY.
STEP 2 — RECORDING ›

Mortgage recorded with County Register IMMEDIATELY after closing.

Puts the world on notice. Perfects the security interest. Establishes priority in the chain. Protects the lender. Protects the borrower.

A recorded mortgage means: everyone who comes after takes subject to it. No surprises. Clean title.

STEP 3 — IF SOLD TO ANOTHER LENDER ›

Lender A sells to Lender B. What must happen:

1. Physical delivery of the ORIGINAL NOTE — endorsed by Lender A to Lender B — “Pay to the order of Lender B” — DATED — SIGNED by authorized officer.

2. Written Assignment of Mortgage — executed by Lender A — delivered to Lender B — RECORDED with County Register — BEFORE any enforcement action.

3. UCC-1 Financing Statement — filed to perfect security interest — names new holder correctly.

RESULT: Clean chain. Lender B holds note + mortgage. Together. Perfected. Recorded. Enforceable.
STEP 4 — IF SECURITIZED ›

Lender sells into a trust. What must happen:

1. Pooling and Servicing Agreement — governs the trust — defines conveyance — defines servicer authority — PUBLIC DOCUMENT — filed with SEC.

2. True Sale — note physically transferred — endorsed in blank OR endorsed to trust specifically — DATED endorsement — delivery to trust custodian — documented.

3. Assignment of Mortgage — recorded in every county where secured property sits — BEFORE trust closes — names trust as assignee.

4. Servicer Appointment — named in PSA — authority defined — limited to PSA terms — cannot exceed PSA authority.

RESULT: Trust holds note + mortgage. Together. Perfected. Recorded. PSA governs everything. Chain is clean from origination to trust to servicer.
STEP 5 — IF BORROWER DEFAULTS ›

What must happen before enforcement:

1. Prove ownership of the NOTE — produce the original — show the endorsement chain — every link documented.

2. Prove ownership of the MORTGAGE — show recorded assignment — chain from originator to trust — every link recorded.

3. Prove SERVICER AUTHORITY — point to PSA — show servicer is named — show authority to enforce.

4. Prove STANDING — holder of note at commencement — United Wholesale v. Smith 2025 — must hold BOTH at filing.

RESULT: Enforcement is clean. Borrower had fair notice. Chain is documented. Court can verify everything.
STEP 6 — ACCOUNTING TO BORROWER ›

Throughout the life of the loan, the borrower is entitled to:

Clear statement of principal balance. Clear statement of interest applied. Clear statement of escrow. Clear statement of fees. Identity of current note holder. Identity of servicer and authority. Response to QWR within 30 days. Response to UCC §9-210 within 14 days.

RESULT: Borrower always knows: who holds the note, who holds the mortgage, what is owed, where payments go.
